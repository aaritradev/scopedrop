# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Freelancers (designers, developers, copywriters, marketers) and solo consultants in India, Southeast Asia, Middle East, and Africa. They work on client projects, spend hours writing briefs and proposals, and need a fast, professional way to turn messy client conversations into signable documents. English-first, mobile-heavy, price-sensitive (₹299-₹799/mo).

## Product Purpose

ScopeDrop turns any unstructured client communication (email, WhatsApp thread, meeting notes) into a complete, professional project onboarding pack: brief, scope, timeline, payment terms. In under 60 seconds. One input. One click. Done.

## Brand Personality

Confident, no fluff, boutique-quality. This is the tool a freelancer uses when they want to look like a serious agency. Voice is direct and assured. Not salesy, not casual. Every word earns its place.

## Anti-references

- Dubsado, HoneyBook: overcomplicated, template-heavy, requires setup
- Generic SaaS landing pages with identical card grids and gradient text
- Over-rounded "friendly" UI: this is sharp, not soft
- AI-slop giveaways: cream/sand backgrounds, sketchy illustrations, stripe borders, glassmorphism

## Design Principles

1. **Boutique clarity** - Every screen feels like a premium document. White space is intentional. The output is the hero.
2. **Confidence, not decoration** - No element exists purely for visual effect. If it doesn't serve the task, cut it.
3. **Frictionless by default** - The fastest path from paste to brief. Zero onboarding. No templates to choose.
4. **Client-ready output** - Everything the freelancer produces (PDF, share link) looks like it came from a top-tier agency.
5. **Mobile-first practicality** - Freelancers work on phones. Every flow works on a 375px viewport without zooming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. 4.5:1 body text contrast, keyboard navigable, reduced motion support. The target audience includes users on mid-range devices in variable lighting conditions.
